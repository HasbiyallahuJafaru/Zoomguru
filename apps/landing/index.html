What ZoomGuru does

Six tools.
One overlay.

ZoomGuru is not a single feature — it is a full AI career toolkit running invisibly on your desktop. Here is everything inside it.

01

Live AI coaching

Speak any interview question and get a structured, role-tailored answer streaming back in under two seconds. Works on any call — Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, in-person. The overlay is invisible to screen share so only you see it.

Ctrl + Shift + L

02

Hands-free auto mode

Toggle on and ZoomGuru listens continuously using voice activity detection. The moment you stop speaking it transcribes and answers — no hotkeys, no clicks. Pure focus on the conversation.

Ctrl + Shift + D

03

Screenshot walk-through

Facing a technical round? Press one key to capture your screen. ZoomGuru reads the coding challenge, system design diagram, or case study and walks you through a full solution — the reasoning, the code, and why it works.

Ctrl + Shift + S

04

AI mock interviewer

Run a full simulated interview for your specific role. The AI generates realistic questions, listens to your spoken answers via microphone, and at the end produces a detailed score report — what you said, how well you said it, and exactly what to sharpen.

05

Document copilot

Upload your CV, a PDF case study, or a PPTX deck and ask questions about it. ZoomGuru reads every page and gives grounded, document-backed answers — not hallucinations. Useful for case prep, cover letter review, and JD analysis.

06

Answer scoring & reports

After every practice session, your coach scores each answer on clarity, depth, relevance, and confidence. You get a written report with specific feedback on every response — not a generic grade, but a breakdown of exactly what to fix.

Hidden from screen share

ZoomGuru uses Electron's setContentProtection API to make the overlay invisible to Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, and any other screen-capture software. The interviewer sees your face. You see everything.

Nothing leaves your session

Questions, answers, and documents live only in memory while the app is open. Close ZoomGuru and everything is gone — no logs, no transcripts, no data used to train any model.

How it works

Before the interview.
During it. After.

Most tools help you prepare. ZoomGuru is there for all three phases — the prep sessions before, the live assist during, and the feedback loop after. Here is what a real workflow looks like.

01

Load the role

Paste the job description and upload your CV. ZoomGuru reads both and uses them to ground every answer it gives you — tailored to the exact company, role, and level, not a generic template.

02

Run a mock interview

Launch the AI interviewer and go through a full simulated session. It generates questions based on the role, listens to your spoken answers, and scores each one in a detailed end-of-session report so you know exactly where you are strong and where you are losing marks.

03

Go live — coached or hands-free

On the day of the real interview, ZoomGuru runs in the background. Press a hotkey to speak a question and get a streamed answer in seconds. Or enable auto mode and it listens continuously — transcribing and answering the moment each question ends, without you lifting a finger. Screenshot a whiteboard or technical problem and get a full walk-through instantly.

Use the copilot between rounds

Have a case study, a PDF brief, or a slide deck to review between interview rounds? Upload it to the Document Copilot and ask questions about the content. ZoomGuru reads every page and gives you grounded, document-backed answers — not guesses.
