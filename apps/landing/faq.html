About ZoomGuru

ZoomGuru is an AI-powered interview coaching app that runs on your desktop. You practise with it before and during your interviews — ask it questions out loud or screenshot a problem, and it coaches you through a strong, well-structured answer tailored to the role you are going for. Think of it as having a brilliant, patient coach available any time you need one.

Google gives you information. ZoomGuru coaches you on how to deliver it. It structures answers the way a strong candidate would actually speak them in an interview — with the right framing, appropriate depth, and language that sounds natural coming from you. It also tailors everything to the specific role you are going for, so you are not reciting a generic answer.

Using the app

Download the app, create an account, and paste in the job description you are preparing for. From there, press Ctrl+Shift+L to speak a question, or Ctrl+Shift+S to screenshot something on your screen. Your coach responds in under two seconds. You can run as many practice sessions as you need before the real interview.

The first words appear in under two seconds. The rest streams in as you read, so you never wait for a full page to load. It feels like a real conversation, which makes practice sessions fast and natural.

There are four main hotkeys:

Ctrl+Shift+L — Speak a question and get a coached answer.
Ctrl+Shift+S — Screenshot your screen and get a walk-through.
Ctrl+Shift+D — Toggle auto mode, which listens continuously without pressing a button.
Ctrl+Shift+H — Hide or show the overlay instantly.
Ctrl+Shift+C — Clear the current answer from the screen.
